From the Hive to Your Home

Founded in 2023, Bee Cream was born from a simple belief: nature already has the answers. We don't need complex chemicals to achieve beautiful skin.

Our journey began in a small family-owned apiary (bee farm) where we witnessed the incredible healing properties of raw honey and royal jelly on our own hands. Workers who handled the honeycombs rarely had dry skin, despite the harsh weather.

We spent two years refining a formula that captures this raw potency in a luxurious cream structure. We work exclusively with ethical beekeepers who prioritize the health of the hive over mass production.

Cruelty Free

We love our bees. No bees are harmed in our harvesting process. We leave enough honey for the colony to thrive through winter.

Sustainability

Our glass jars are 100% recyclable, and our packaging is made from recycled materials.
